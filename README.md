# Troubleshooting Tickets

 ### Troubleshooting Methodology


This project demonstrates a structured Help Desk troubleshooting process for managing and resolving IT incidents in a simulated enterprise environment.

It highlights core IT support skills, including ticket management, incident response, customer service, troubleshooting methodology, root cause analysis, documentation, escalation, and SLA/time constraint awareness.

---

## 🚦 Incident Priority System

Incidents are prioritized based on business impact and urgency using a three-tier system:

- 🔴 High (Critical Priority)
- 🟡 Medium (Normal Priority)
- 🟢 Low (Low Priority)

---

## 🔄 5-Step Troubleshooting Process

### 1. Triage & Prioritize

- Assess severity, urgency, and business impact
- Determine scope (single user, group, or organization-wide)
- Categorize the issue (hardware, software, network, access, etc.)
- Acknowledge the ticket and assign priority (SLA-aware)

---

### 2. Gather Information

- Collect key details from the user:
    - Error messages or screenshots
    - Steps leading to the issue
    - Recent changes (updates, installs, password changes)
    - Device, OS, or application details

---

### 3. Diagnose & Investigate

- Analyze symptoms and possible causes
- Check logs, systems, or known issues
- Search the knowledge base or past tickets
- Reproduce the issue when possible

---

### 4. Resolve or Escalate (SLA-Aware)

- Apply basic fixes (restart, reset, configuration changes)
- Escalate when:
    - The issue is outside the scope or permissions
    - Advanced technical support is required
    - SLA/time constraints require faster resolution
- Provide full context when escalating (actions taken, findings, impact)

---

### 5. Verify & Document

- Confirm the issue is fully resolved with the user
- Test functionality and check for side effects
- Document:
    - Root cause
    - Steps taken
    - Final resolution or escalation details
- Close the ticket and ensure correct categorization

---

## 🧠 Key Operational Principles

- SLA/time targets guide response, resolution, and escalation priorities
- Escalate when issues exceed scope, require higher expertise, or risk SLA breaches, including the full context of work performed
- Track recurring incidents, update the knowledge base, and identify systemic issues
- Communicate clearly and consistently with users throughout the ticket lifecycle
- Outcome: faster resolution, SLA compliance, proper escalation handling, and consistent documentation
- Close the ticket with proper resolution
